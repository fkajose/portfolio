About Me

I'm Farook Ajose, a chemical engineer and data analyst interested in solving complex problems with engineering, data, and technology.

I started in chemical engineering, where I developed a foundation in process analysis, modelling, optimisation, and systems thinking. Since then, my work has expanded into environmental and social due diligence, ESG analysis, data analytics, automation, and AI-assisted systems.

A recurring theme across my work is finding processes that are unnecessarily manual, fragmented, or difficult to analyse, then building better ways to handle them.

I've built analytical models for industrial energy decisions, automated regulatory research for environmental and social due diligence, developed workflow systems for organisational operations, and used Python and statistical methods to investigate real-world problems.

I'm particularly interested in work at the intersection of:

  • Engineering & systems — process modelling, optimisation, environmental systems, and quantitative problem-solving
  • Data & analytics — Python, SQL, statistical analysis, visualisation, and machine learning
  • Automation & AI — turning repetitive knowledge work into reliable, usable systems
  • Environmental & ESG — environmental and social risk, regulatory analysis, sustainability, and infrastructure
  • Research — process systems engineering, environmental process engineering, and data-driven approaches to complex systems

My academic background is in Chemical Engineering from the University of Lagos, where I graduated with First Class honours. I'm also pursuing an MSc in Financial Engineering, expanding my quantitative background into statistics, optimisation, and computational finance.

This portfolio documents some of the problems I've worked on, the systems I've built, and the reasoning behind them.

I'm interested in problems that sit between disciplines. Those tend to be the ones that don't come with a neat spreadsheet already waiting for you.
